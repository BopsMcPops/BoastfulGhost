Liveline with Mason

A detailed account of my journey for a silly mousepad


4/6/2026

It all started on Friday, April 3rd. I had just finished listening to the Greyhounds game on Rock 101. After doing all of the work to get the radio upstairs I decided I'd keep listening but I turned it to 99.5 Yes FM. Liveline was on, and they played a secret sound... Natalie called in and did not get it, I called in with a really close answer. I guessed a drawer closing! But it wasn't specific enough! Someone else got it... So today I listened for the secret sound. Natalie called in and requested a song! But she was embarrassed to be on the radio. I called in for the secret sound and got the answer right! But I couldn't get through to their phones! Later though I called in and I requested a song so that was cool. But tomorrow night I'm going to be listening and I'm going to try my best to guess the secret sound and win that silly mousepad!
